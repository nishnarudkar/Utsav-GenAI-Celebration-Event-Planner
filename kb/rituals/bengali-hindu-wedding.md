# Bengali Hindu wedding

A Bengali Hindu wedding usually spans three to four days: blessings and turmeric before the wedding, the wedding night itself (the *Biye*), and the welcome and feast at the groom's home afterwards. Families differ in which rituals they keep and in their order, and the family priest (*purohit*) decides the exact timings, often from the almanac (*panjika*). Durations below are typical ranges for planning, not rules.

## Aashirbaad

- **Key:** aashirbaad
- **When:** One to a few days before the wedding; separately at the bride's and the groom's homes
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride or groom, elders from the other family, priest (optional)
- **Items:** Paddy (dhaan), durba grass, sandalwood paste, gifts from the visiting elders, sweets
- **Significance:** Elders of the other family formally bless the bride or groom, accepting them into the family.

## Dodhi Mangal

- **Key:** dodhi_mangal
- **When:** Before sunrise on the wedding day
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom (at his own home), married women of the family
- **Items:** Curd, flattened rice (chira), sweets, water from a pond or river if the family follows that custom
- **Significance:** A last light meal before the bride and groom fast for the rest of the wedding day.

## Gaye Holud

- **Key:** gaye_holud
- **When:** Morning of the wedding day; first at the groom's home, then at the bride's
- **Typical duration:** 90 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, married women of both families, close friends
- **Items:** Turmeric paste, mustard oil, gift trays (tattva) sent from the groom's family, fish (traditionally rohu), sweets, a yellow saree for the bride
- **Significance:** Turmeric touched to the groom is carried to the bride, linking the two families before the wedding.

## Shankha Porana

- **Key:** shankha_porana
- **When:** Morning of the wedding day, usually alongside Gaye Holud
- **Typical duration:** 20 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, married women of the bride's family
- **Items:** Shankha (white conch-shell bangles), pola (red bangles), a small bowl of water or milk
- **Significance:** The bride wears the white and red bangles that traditionally mark a married Bengali woman.

## Bor Boron

- **Key:** bor_boron
- **When:** Evening of the wedding day, when the groom's party (bor jatri) arrives
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Groom, bride's mother and married women of the bride's family, groom's party
- **Items:** Welcome platter (boron dala) with lamp, paddy, durba grass and sweets, conch shell (shankha) to blow, ulu-dhwani by the women
- **Significance:** The bride's family welcomes the groom and his party at the wedding venue.

## Saat Paak and Shubho Drishti

- **Key:** saat_paak_shubho_drishti
- **When:** Start of the wedding ceremony, after the groom is seated
- **Typical duration:** 20 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, bride's brothers or male cousins
- **Items:** Wooden seat (piri) to carry the bride, two betel leaves, flower decorations
- **Significance:** The bride, carried on a piri, circles the groom seven times, then lowers the betel leaves for their first formal look.

## Mala Badal

- **Key:** mala_badal
- **When:** Immediately after Shubho Drishti
- **Typical duration:** 10 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, family members who lift the bride
- **Items:** Two flower garlands (often rajanigandha or marigold)
- **Significance:** The couple exchange garlands, traditionally three times, as a sign of mutual acceptance.

## Sampradan

- **Key:** sampradan
- **When:** At the wedding altar (chhadnatola) after Mala Badal
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, bride's father or another elder who gives her away, priest
- **Items:** Sacred thread, copper plate, mango leaves, new clothes, ghee lamp
- **Significance:** An elder of the bride's family formally entrusts her to the groom with mantras.

## Yagna and Anjali

- **Key:** yagna_anjali
- **When:** After Sampradan
- **Typical duration:** 45 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, bride's brother, priest
- **Items:** Sacred fire setup (havan kund), ghee, wood, puffed rice (khoi), flowers
- **Significance:** The couple make offerings into the sacred fire, with the bride's brother passing the puffed rice.

## Saptapadi and Sindoor Daan

- **Key:** saptapadi_sindoor_daan
- **When:** After the Yagna, ending the main ceremony
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, priest
- **Items:** Sindoor (vermilion), a new saree or veil (lojjabostro) to cover the bride's head, seven betel leaves or small stones for the steps
- **Significance:** The couple take seven steps together; the groom then applies sindoor to the bride's hair parting.

## Bashor Raat

- **Key:** bashor_raat
- **When:** The night of the wedding, after dinner
- **Typical duration:** 180 minutes
- **Participants:** Couple, cousins and friends from both families
- **Items:** A decorated room with mattresses and bolsters, snacks, tea, music, games
- **Significance:** The couple stay up through the night with younger family members, with songs, games and teasing.

## Bidaai and Kanakanjali

- **Key:** bidaai
- **When:** Morning after the wedding
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, bride's mother and family, groom
- **Items:** A handful of rice and coins, decorated car
- **Significance:** Leaving home, the bride throws rice over her shoulder to her mother, wishing prosperity for the home she leaves.

## Bou Boron

- **Key:** bou_boron
- **When:** On arrival at the groom's home after Bidaai
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, groom's mother and married women of his family
- **Items:** Plate of alta (red dye) and milk to step into, live fish (in some families), welcome platter, conch shell
- **Significance:** The groom's mother welcomes the bride, who steps into the home leaving red footprints for good fortune.

## Bou Bhaat

- **Key:** bou_bhaat
- **When:** One or two days after the wedding, at the groom's home; often combined with the reception
- **Typical duration:** 240 minutes
- **Participants:** Couple, groom's family, guests from both families
- **Items:** Feast for guests, plate of rice and a new saree for the groom to give the bride, serving utensils
- **Significance:** The groom promises to provide food and clothing; the bride serves rice to the family for the first time.
