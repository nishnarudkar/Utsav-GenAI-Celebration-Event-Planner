# Tamil Brahmin wedding

A Tamil Brahmin wedding usually takes place over two days: the evening before the muhurtham, when the groom is received and the engagement is formalised, and the morning of the muhurtham, when the thaali is tied. Practice differs between families and communities (for example between Iyer and Iyengar families), so check with the family's vadhyar (priest), who also fixes the muhurtham time. Durations below are typical planning ranges.

## Panda Kaal Muhurtham

- **Key:** panda_kaal_muhurtham
- **When:** One or two days before the wedding
- **Typical duration:** 45 minutes
- **Participants:** Both families' elders, vadhyar
- **Items:** Bamboo pole, turmeric, kumkum, mango leaves, flowers, coconut
- **Significance:** A bamboo pole is worshipped and set up so the wedding preparations go ahead without obstacles.

## Sumangali Prarthanai

- **Key:** sumangali_prarthanai
- **When:** A few days before the wedding, at the bride's home (some groom's families hold one too)
- **Typical duration:** 120 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, married women of the family (an odd number is customary in many families), bride's mother
- **Items:** Nine-yard sarees for the honoured women, flowers, turmeric, kumkum, a traditional meal served on banana leaves
- **Significance:** Married women of the family are honoured and their blessings sought for the bride's married life.

## Vratham

- **Key:** vratham
- **When:** Morning of the day before the wedding; separately for bride and groom
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride or groom, parents, vadhyar
- **Items:** Sacred thread (kaappu) for the wrist, homam materials, new clothes
- **Significance:** The bride and groom each pray to the deities and tie a protective thread before the wedding rituals begin.

## Janavasam

- **Key:** janavasam
- **When:** Evening before the muhurtham
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** Groom, groom's family and friends, bride's family receiving them
- **Items:** Decorated car or procession arrangement, nadaswaram musicians, flowers, aarti plate
- **Significance:** The groom is taken in procession to the venue and formally received by the bride's family.

## Nichayathartham

- **Key:** nichayathartham
- **When:** Evening before the muhurtham, after Janavasam
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, both families, vadhyar
- **Items:** Lagna patrikai (wedding agreement), saree for the bride from the groom's family, fruits, betel leaves and nuts, flowers, coconut
- **Significance:** The families formally read out and accept the wedding agreement; the bride receives a saree from the groom's family.

## Kasi Yatra

- **Key:** kasi_yatra
- **When:** Morning of the muhurtham, before the main ceremony
- **Typical duration:** 20 minutes
- **Participants:** Groom, bride's father, both families
- **Items:** Umbrella, walking stick, fan, slippers, a copy of a sacred text, coconut
- **Significance:** In a playful ritual the groom sets off for a life of study; the bride's father persuades him to marry instead.

## Maalai Maatral

- **Key:** maalai_maatral
- **When:** After Kasi Yatra
- **Typical duration:** 15 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, maternal uncles who lift them
- **Items:** Two flower garlands
- **Significance:** The couple exchange garlands three times, lifted on their uncles' shoulders amid friendly competition.

## Oonjal

- **Key:** oonjal
- **When:** After Maalai Maatral
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, married women of both families
- **Items:** Decorated swing, cups of milk and bananas, coloured rice balls (pidi suttral), lamps, women singing oonjal songs
- **Significance:** The couple sit on a swing while women sing and circle coloured rice balls to ward off the evil eye.

## Kanyadaanam

- **Key:** kanyadaanam
- **When:** At the mandapam after Oonjal
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, bride's parents, vadhyar
- **Items:** Coconut, nine-yard saree (koorai) for the bride, water vessel
- **Significance:** The bride's parents give her hand to the groom; she then changes into the koorai saree.

## Mangalya Dharanam

- **Key:** mangalya_dharanam
- **When:** At the muhurtham time fixed by the vadhyar
- **Typical duration:** 15 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, groom's sister, vadhyar, nadaswaram musicians
- **Items:** Thaali (mangalyam) on a turmeric-coated thread, plate of akshatai (rice with turmeric) for guests to shower
- **Significance:** The groom ties the thaali while the music rises (getti melam); guests shower akshatai as blessing.

## Paanigrahanam and Saptapadi

- **Key:** paanigrahanam_saptapadi
- **When:** Immediately after Mangalya Dharanam
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, vadhyar
- **Items:** Sacred fire setup (homam), ghee, samidhs (wood sticks)
- **Significance:** The groom takes the bride's hand and they walk seven steps around the sacred fire, each step a vow.

## Ammi Midhithal and Arundhati Darshanam

- **Key:** ammi_midhithal
- **When:** After Saptapadi
- **Typical duration:** 15 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, vadhyar
- **Items:** Grinding stone (ammi), toe rings (metti)
- **Significance:** The bride places her foot on a grinding stone, symbolising steadiness; the groom puts toe rings on her and shows her the Arundhati star.

## Laaja Homam

- **Key:** laaja_homam
- **When:** After Ammi Midhithal
- **Typical duration:** 20 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, bride's brother, vadhyar
- **Items:** Puffed rice (pori), ghee, sacred fire
- **Significance:** The bride's brother fills her hands with puffed rice, which the couple offer into the fire together.

## Nalangu

- **Key:** nalangu
- **When:** Afternoon of the wedding day
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** Couple, cousins and friends from both families
- **Items:** Coconut to roll, papad to break, turmeric and kumkum, flowers, a bowl for a ring-finding game
- **Significance:** Light-hearted games between the couple help both families relax and get to know each other.

## Grihapravesam

- **Key:** grihapravesam
- **When:** On arrival at the groom's home after the wedding
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Bride, groom, groom's mother and family
- **Items:** Aarti plate, vessel of rice at the threshold, lamp
- **Significance:** The groom's family welcomes the bride into her new home.
