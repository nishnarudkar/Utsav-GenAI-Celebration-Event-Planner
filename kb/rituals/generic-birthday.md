# Birthday celebration (any tradition)

A typical Indian birthday celebration mixes a family blessing, a cake-cutting and a party for friends. Many families add a short religious element (a puja, a gurdwara visit, a mass or a prayer) in the morning; ask rather than assume which, if any. The sequence below is a common shape for an evening party of 2–4 hours. Durations are typical planning ranges.

## Morning blessing

- **Key:** morning_blessing
- **When:** Morning of the birthday, at home or at a place of worship
- **Typical duration:** 45 minutes
- **Participants:** Birthday person, parents, grandparents and elders
- **Items:** Lamp or candle, flowers, sweets, new clothes for the birthday person, offering as per the family's practice
- **Significance:** Elders bless the birthday person; many families also offer a prayer of thanks for the year.

## Guest welcome

- **Key:** guest_welcome
- **When:** Start of the party
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Hosts, birthday person, guests
- **Items:** Welcome drinks, name tags or a guest book, photo backdrop, gift table
- **Significance:** Guests settle in, greet the birthday person and get photographed.

## Games and activities

- **Key:** games_activities
- **When:** After guests have arrived
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** Guests (often grouped by age), an anchor or entertainer
- **Items:** Games props, prizes, music system, activity corner (art, magic show, tattoo or balloon artist for children)
- **Significance:** Keeps guests of all ages engaged; especially important at children's parties.

## Aarti and cake-cutting

- **Key:** aarti_cake_cutting
- **When:** Peak of the party, when everyone is present
- **Typical duration:** 20 minutes
- **Participants:** Birthday person, family, all guests
- **Items:** Cake, candles, knife, lighter, plates and napkins; aarti plate with lamp and sweets for families who do an aarti
- **Significance:** The centrepiece moment: candles, the birthday song and the first slice, often fed to the parents.

## Dinner or high tea

- **Key:** dinner
- **When:** After cake-cutting
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** All guests
- **Items:** Catering or home-cooked food sized to the guest count, separate options for children, water and serving staff
- **Significance:** The meal is usually the largest single cost; plan quantities from the confirmed guest count.

## Return gifts and farewell

- **Key:** return_gifts
- **When:** As guests leave
- **Typical duration:** 20 minutes
- **Participants:** Hosts, birthday person, guests
- **Items:** Return gifts (one per guest or per family), thank-you cards, packed sweets or cake slices
- **Significance:** A thank-you to each guest; plan one return gift per child guest or per family.
