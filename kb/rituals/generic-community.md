# Neighbourhood community festival (any tradition)

A typical housing-society or neighbourhood festival is run by volunteers over a day or a weekend, usually with an opening, a cultural programme and a shared meal. Any religious element depends on the community and the occasion, so ask rather than assume. The sequence below is a common shape; durations are typical planning ranges.

## Volunteer briefing

- **Key:** volunteer_briefing
- **When:** Evening before the event, at the venue
- **Typical duration:** 60 minutes
- **Participants:** Organising committee, volunteers
- **Items:** Duty roster, volunteer badges, contact list, site map, first-aid kit
- **Significance:** Everyone learns their duty, the exits and who to call if something goes wrong.

## Opening

- **Key:** community_opening
- **When:** Start of the event
- **Typical duration:** 45 minutes
- **Participants:** Organising committee, residents, invited guests
- **Items:** Lamp or ribbon, flowers, microphone, welcome banner
- **Significance:** The committee welcomes everyone and thanks sponsors and volunteers.

## Cultural programme

- **Key:** cultural_programme
- **When:** After the opening
- **Typical duration:** 180 minutes
- **Participants:** Residents, children, local performers
- **Items:** Stage, sound system, running order, seating, participation certificates
- **Significance:** Neighbours of all ages perform, which is what makes the day feel like everyone's own.

## Community meal

- **Key:** community_meal
- **When:** Lunch or dinner, depending on the event
- **Typical duration:** 120 minutes
- **Participants:** All attendees, serving volunteers
- **Items:** Serving counters, plates, drinking water, separate waste bins
- **Significance:** Sharing a meal brings people together, whatever their background.

## Closing

- **Key:** community_closing
- **When:** End of the event
- **Typical duration:** 30 minutes
- **Participants:** Organising committee, volunteers, residents
- **Items:** Thank-you note, prizes, announcements for the way out
- **Significance:** The committee thanks everyone and guides the crowd out in an orderly way.

## Clean-up

- **Key:** community_cleanup
- **When:** Morning after the event
- **Typical duration:** 120 minutes
- **Participants:** Volunteers, housekeeping staff
- **Items:** Gloves, waste bags, recycling sacks, cleaning supplies
- **Significance:** The grounds are handed back clean, which keeps neighbours and the local authority on side for next year.
